Your Welcome Package

A note before you arrive

You are welcome exactly where you are

Whatever you are carrying — tiredness, questions, hope, grief, quiet curiosity — you are welcome to bring it. Meals will be prepared, the rooms are ready, and the schedule is gentle. Please arrive with whatever willingness you have and let the rest unfold.

The venue

Enchanted Hills Napa sits on 311 wooded acres atop Mt. Veeder, just west of Napa — a quiet property with a heated outdoor pool (April–October), a small pond, community rooms with kitchen and lounge space, and miles of paved footpaths and wooded trails. Retreat dates: Saturday, September 19 – Sunday, September 20, 2026.

A short list

What to bring

  • Flashlight or headlamp — one per person, mandatory. Paths are unlit and the campus is very dark at night.
  • Any medications you take regularly, plus anything you may occasionally need
  • Toiletries, sunscreen, refillable water bottle, bug repellent
  • Warm layers — Napa hills are cool in the mornings and evenings
  • Closed-toe walking shoes (poison oak & ticks on the trails); comfortable clothes for meditation; pajamas
  • Swimsuit + pool towel only if you plan to use the pool or pond
  • Your own meditation cushion, folded blanket, or shawl if you prefer to sit on the floor — cushions are not provided
  • An open, receptive heart

Provided for you: meals, lodging, one bed-linen set per bed, one bath towel and one hand towel per guest, session materials, and campus Wi-Fi.

Finding us

Getting to Enchanted Hills

3410 Mount Veeder Road, Napa, CA 94558 — use Google Maps. Some navigation systems try to send you into a neighboring driveway; don't turn until you see the sign and address markers for 3410. A wooden gate with a call box between two yellow pillars marks the entrance — push the call button to reach the operator.

Driving directions

The most common route from the Bay Area: Highway 29 → Redwood Road exit → follow Redwood Road west (it becomes Mount Veeder Road) → our driveway is directly off Mount Veeder — turn right at the Enchanted Hills sign. Coming from Sonoma, Trinity Road from Highway 12 also works. Cell reception weakens on the final approach — save the directions before you leave home.

Parking & carpooling

Main lot in front of the Welcome Pavilion; EV charging on site (ChargePoint, 4-hour session limit). Redwood Park & Ride (3416 Solano Avenue, Napa) is a convenient carpool meeting point — reply to the retreat email if you'd like to be connected with others coming from your area.

Arrival

Retreat check-in opens at 9:00 am Saturday, September 19. Aim for 9:00–10:00 am so we can begin together with the meet & greet at 10:00 am. When you drive up, proceed to the Welcome Pavilion before parking — the driver signs in for everyone in the vehicle (Enchanted Hills collects this in case of emergency).

Cabin rooms open at 4 pm — leave luggage at the Welcome Pavilion in the morning and settle in later. The property gate operates 8 am – 10 pm; tell Guest Services in advance if you'll arrive outside those hours.

Safety

  • Wildlife. The property has rattlesnakes and scorpions — do not approach; back away and alert a staff member. Please don't feed or approach the deer and turkeys either.
  • Open fires. Not permitted outside pre-arranged, staffed fire circles — Napa fire risk is real.
  • After dark. Flashlight always, walk with a buddy, watch for animals.
  • Emergency. Call 911 first, then Guest Services. Emergency response can be 30 minutes away.

Guidelines & policies

  • Phones on silent during sessions
  • No photographs of other participants without their permission
  • Silence after evening kirtan
  • No pets (service dogs welcome)
  • No smoking or vaping indoors
  • No alcohol use while attending the retreat
  • No drug use; no nudity on campus
  • Kindness first — with ourselves, with each other, with whatever arises
