Enchanted Hills sits on 311 wooded acres atop Mt. Veeder, just west of Napa — a quiet property with a swimming pool, a small pond, and miles of walking trails. It is a rare pocket of stillness within an easy drive of the Bay Area.

Enchanted Hills is a property of LightHouse for the Blind and Visually Impaired. Since 1950 the site has hosted Enchanted Hills Camp, a summer program for blind and low-vision campers of all ages. Hosting our retreat directly supports that mission.

Amenities on the property

  • Heated outdoor swimming pool — open 10 am – 8 pm; the season runs April through October, weather permitting. No lifeguard on duty; bring your own pool towel.
  • Small pond — open 10 am – 5 pm; paddle-boat rental available on request through Guest Services.
  • Hiking trails — miles of gentle wooded trails and paved footpaths connecting every part of the campus.
  • Community rooms — lounge and kitchen spaces you can use during free time.
  • Gathering spaces — indoor and outdoor spots for our sessions and kirtan.

Bring a swimsuit and closed-toe walking shoes if you'd like to use the pool, pond, or trails during free time.

Finding us

Getting to Enchanted Hills

Enchanted Hills Napa
3410 Mount Veeder Road, Napa, CA 94558

Use Google Maps for directions — some in-car navigation systems will try to send you into a neighboring driveway. Do not turn until you see the sign and address markers for 3410. You'll see a wooden gate and a call box between two yellow pillars — push the call button to speak with the gate operator.

Driving directions

From Napa via Redwood Road. Take Highway 29 to the Redwood Road exit. Follow Redwood Road west; it becomes Mount Veeder Road as it climbs. Our driveway is directly off Mount Veeder — turn right at the Enchanted Hills sign.

From Napa via Oakville Grade. From Highway 29, take Oakville Grade west for ~3.6 miles (past the Lakoya fire department on the right). Turn left onto Mount Veeder Road and follow it ~2.7 miles to our gate.

From Santa Rosa / Sonoma via Trinity Road. From Highway 12, turn onto Trinity Road for 5.5 miles (it becomes Dry Creek Road). Continue 2 miles, turn right onto Mount Veeder Road, and drive 3 miles to our gate on the left.

Cell reception weakens on the final approach. Please save these directions and phone numbers before you leave home.

Carpooling

If you'd like to share a ride, reply to the retreat email and we'll connect you with others coming from your area. The Redwood Park & Ride (3416 Solano Avenue, Napa) is a convenient meeting point at the base of Mt. Veeder.

Parking

Our main parking lot sits beneath a solar-panel structure in front of the Welcome Pavilion — ~55 guest spaces. 14 EV charging stations are on site (ChargePoint); sessions are limited to 4 hours per vehicle. If the main lot fills up, auxiliary parking is available for ~50 additional vehicles.

Arrival & check-in

Retreat check-in with our team opens at 9:00 am Saturday, September 19. Please plan to arrive between 9:00 and 10:00 am so we can begin together with the meet & greet at 10:00 am.

When you drive up, proceed directly to the Welcome Pavilion before parking. The driver will be asked to sign in (name, phone, email, vehicle make + plate, and the names of anyone riding with you) — Enchanted Hills collects this in case of emergency.

Cabin room check-in opens at 4:00 pm — you're welcome to leave luggage at the Welcome Pavilion in the morning and settle into your room later in the afternoon.

The property gate is open only from 8 am to 10 pm. If you'll be arriving outside those hours, tell Guest Services in advance.

A short list

What to bring

  • Flashlight or headlamp — one per person, mandatory. The campus is extremely dark at night and paths are unlit.
  • Insect / bug repellent — recommended for evenings outdoors
  • Any medication you take regularly, plus anything you may occasionally need (pain reliever, allergy meds, digestive aids, etc.)
  • Personal toiletries — shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • Refillable water bottle — refill stations are throughout campus
  • Sun protection — sunscreen, sunglasses, hat or visor
  • A small backpack or daypack, in case luggage transport is delayed on arrival
  • Swimsuit + pool towel if you'd like to use the pool or pond (bath towels are provided; pool towels are not)
  • Your own meditation cushion, folded blanket, or shawl if you prefer to sit on the floor — cushions are not provided on site
  • A journal and pen for reflections
  • An open, receptive heart

What to wear

  • Hiking, tennis, or walking shoes — closed-toe is best. The Mt. Veeder area has poison oak and ticks.
  • Long pants and long socks for the trails
  • Shorts, T-shirts, sweatshirt, jacket — dress in layers; Napa hills can be cool morning and evening
  • Pajamas, slippers or slides for your room
  • Comfortable clothes for sitting, meditation, and gentle movement

Provided for you: meals, lodging, one set of bed linens per bed, one bath towel and one hand towel per guest, session materials, and campus Wi-Fi. Pool towels and meditation cushions are not provided — please bring your own if you'd like to use them.

Meals

Meals are served in the Forest Commons Dining Hall (buffet or family-style). Our retreat schedule tracks the standard house meal times:

  • Breakfast — 8:30 to 9:30 am
  • Lunch — 12:30 to 1:30 pm
  • Dinner — 6:00 to 7:00 pm

Please help keep the dining hall running smoothly by bussing your own table and using the compost, recycling, and trash bins as labelled. Do not keep or eat food in your cabin — it attracts wildlife.

Getting around campus

Paved walking paths connect every building — please use them rather than the main access road. Personal vehicles are not allowed past the parking lot. If you need a shuttle between buildings (mobility, luggage, weather), Guest Services can arrange one; on-campus transportation runs 8 am to 10 pm.

Quiet hours & gate

Quiet hours are 10 pm to 8 am. Amplified sound and on-campus transportation both stop at 10 pm, and the entry gate closes at 10 pm and reopens at 8 am. If your plans include leaving or returning outside those hours, arrange it with Guest Services in advance.

Safety — wildlife, fire, water

  • Wildlife. The property has rattlesnakes and scorpions. Do not approach — back away and alert a staff member. Please do not feed or approach the deer and turkeys either; they can become aggressive.
  • Open fires. Not permitted outside pre-arranged, staffed fire circles — Napa's fire risk is real. If you notice smoke or flame anywhere unexpected, call Guest Services immediately.
  • Pool & pond. Pool 10 am – 8 pm; pond 10 am – 5 pm. No lifeguard on duty; use only with a buddy. Bring your own pool towel.
  • After dark. Carry your flashlight, take a buddy, and watch for animals whenever you're walking the property after sunset.
  • Emergency. Dial 911 first, then Guest Services (415) 526-7170. Emergency services can be 30 minutes away.

Property policies at a glance

  • No pets — service dogs welcome and must remain with their handler at all times.
  • No smoking or vaping inside buildings. Three designated outdoor smoking areas: end of the Lodge, gravel next to Forest Commons, and Hillside Commons at the top of the stairs.
  • Alcohol — 21+ only, in private cabin areas and the dining hall.
  • No drug use.
  • No nudity anywhere on campus.
  • Bathrooms are on a septic system. Please put personal hygiene products (including flushable wipes) in the trash, not the toilet.
  • Gratuities — the Enchanted Hills team is mission-driven and doesn't accept tips. If you feel inspired, donation QR codes are around the property at common locations.

A gentle framework

Guidelines for our time together

  • Phones on silent. Please keep phones tucked away during sessions. You are welcome to step outside if you need to take a call.
  • Photos between participants. Please refrain from photographing other participants without their permission. The retreat is a private container.
  • Photos taken by our team. Photographs and short video clips may be taken during the retreat by Sattva Path Collective and used on our website (sattvapathcollective.com) and social media. If you would prefer not to appear in any images, please let us know at check-in and we will make sure you are not included.
  • Silence between evening kirtan and morning session. A soft period of quiet after 9:00 pm helps everyone rest well.
  • Dietary needs. Vegetarian meals are provided. If you shared a dietary note at registration, we have it on file.
  • Movement is optional. Every practice is offered as an invitation. Rest is also a practice.
  • Kindness first. With ourselves, with each other, with whatever arises.
