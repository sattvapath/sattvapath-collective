Privacy Policy

Effective July 27, 2026

Sattva Path Collective LLC ("Sattva Path Collective," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Sattva Path Collective LLC is a California limited liability company offering spiritual guidance, meditation, kirtan gatherings, and retreats. Retreats are held at Enchanted Hills Napa; other sessions may be held online or at other locations.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact inquiries — when you use our contact form we collect your name, email, phone number, and message.
  • Retreat registration — for each participant we collect first and last name, age, gender, T-shirt size, email, phone, accommodation preference, and emergency contact name and phone. We also record whether you accepted our liability agreement.
  • "What's on Your Heart" posts — the message text you submit (which is displayed publicly on that page), and an anonymous browser identifier stored in your browser's local storage. That identifier lets you edit or delete your own posts later; it is not linked to any name or email.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your card or bank account numbers. We receive a payment confirmation and a Stripe reference so we can associate payment with a registration.
  • Automatic information — IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited. This is collected via standard web-server logs and via the Meta Pixel on pages that use it (currently the events-retreat and register pages).

3. How we use your information

  • Fulfill retreat registrations and communicate about events you have booked.
  • Respond to your contact inquiries.
  • Coordinate check-in, room assignment, and emergency contact with our retreat venue partner.
  • Send you the welcome package and other event materials.
  • Understand how our site is used and improve our services and marketing.
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

4. How we share your information

We share personal information only as needed to run our services:

  • Enchanted Hills Napa — our retreat venue. For each retreat participant we share the name, email, phone, room choice, dietary or accessibility notes, and emergency contact so the venue can check you in and reach a contact in case of emergency.
  • Stripe — for payment processing. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook / Instagram) — Meta Pixel loads on the events-retreat and register pages and reports pageview data to Meta for advertising analytics. See Meta's privacy policy.
  • Service providers — hosting (GitHub Pages), email delivery, and similar operational vendors, only to the extent needed to run our site and services.
  • Legal — if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sattva Path Collective, our participants, or others.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies, local storage, and tracking

  • Meta Pixel — sets cookies and reports data to Meta. To opt out, adjust ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account, or block third-party cookies in your browser.
  • Local storage — used for the "What's on Your Heart" browser identifier and to save your registration form draft as you fill it out. Cleared when you clear your browser storage.
  • Server logs — standard web-request logs kept by our hosting providers.

6. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed:

  • Registration and payment records: typically seven years, to meet tax and accounting requirements.
  • Contact inquiries: up to two years.
  • "What's on Your Heart" posts: until you delete them or the feature is retired.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by emailing us at Sattvapathcollective@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time. Some information (for example, records of a completed payment) must be retained to meet legal or tax obligations even after a deletion request.

8. Children

Our retreats and services are intended for adults 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect about you, the right to request deletion of that information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising other than as described in the Meta Pixel section above. To exercise these rights, contact us at Sattvapathcollective@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top. Material changes will be highlighted where practical.
